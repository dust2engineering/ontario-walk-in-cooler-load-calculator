Method and honesty notes

Heatcraft engineering-manual method: transmission (U = k/thickness, floor against 55 °F ground), infiltration (air-change tables by box volume; heavy use ×2, long-term storage ×0.6), product load (freezing point, specific heats and latent heat from the manual's Table 7 — ranges are used at their midpoints), motors at 75,000 BTU/HP/24h, lights, people by box temperature, glass doors at 31,200 BTU/door/24h, then a 10% safety factor. The heat removed per cubic foot of infiltrating air is computed psychrometrically (the manual's Table 6 method); it reproduces the manual's worked anchor of ≈ 3.49 BTU/ft³ for an 85 °F ambient / −20 °F box. Respiration heat for fresh produce is not included — no verified table was available, and this tool does not invent one.
